Model the avalanche (highest interest first) or snowball (smallest balance first) method and compare which one clears your full debt stack faster.

AI Coach module Pro

Ask anything
about your money.

The AI Coach is powered by your own API key (OpenAI or Anthropic) — meaning FincWin never routes your financial conversations through its own servers. You control the cost. You control the model. Your data stays yours.

Bring your own API key.
We don't see the conversation.

Your questions, your financial context, and the AI's responses pass directly between your browser and the AI provider you choose. FincWin acts as the interface — not the intermediary. There's no FincWin server reading your salary or your debt balance.

Use Claude, GPT-4, or any compatible model. Pay the provider directly at API rates — typically a fraction of a cent per question.

Security module

Your data, locked to you.

PIN lock, AES-256 passphrase encryption, biometric unlock, auto-lock on inactivity. Security features available on all plans — because protecting your financial data shouldn't be a premium feature.

Encrypted at rest.
Never transmitted.

When you enable passphrase encryption, your entire financial data store is encrypted using AES-256-GCM with a key derived from your passphrase via PBKDF2 (310,000 iterations). The key is never stored — it's derived fresh from your passphrase each session.

The PIN lock uses a separate session key mechanism — meaning one 6-digit PIN per browser tab session, with auto-lock after your configured inactivity period.
