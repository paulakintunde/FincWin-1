Honest assessment

Where each app wins

FincWin wins on:

Privacy-first design, lowest price, permanent free plan, offline capability, gamification, AI coaching with BYOK, lifetime option, and the most complete feature set outside of YNAB.

YNAB wins on:

Native mobile apps, automatic bank sync quality, established community, and educational resources. Best choice if bank sync is essential and price is secondary.

Mint/Credit Karma wins on:

Free access. If you're comfortable with your data being monetised for credit targeting and don't need active budgeting features, free has value.

Monarch Money wins on:

Net worth tracking depth, investment portfolio views, and household/partner sharing. Good choice for couples managing finances together.
