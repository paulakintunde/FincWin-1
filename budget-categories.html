Why 14?

The right number of categories
is not 3, and not 40.

01

Too few hides the detail

Three categories — Fixed, Variable, Savings — works at the macro level but buries every pattern inside "Variable." You can't see whether it's food, transport, or subscriptions that's the problem. You can't fix what you can't see.

02

Too many creates friction

A 40-category budget means 40 decisions per expense. Should this coffee be Dining or Personal? That friction is why people stop logging. An empty budget is worse than a rough one. Fourteen is the sweet spot.

03

14 is deliberate

Enough to make spending patterns visible. Few enough to keep categorisation fast. Every one of FincWin's 14 categories has a reason to exist separately — and together they cover how money actually moves through most households.
