Everything FincWin does,
explained.

This guide covers every feature with step-by-step instructions. Use the sidebar to jump to any section. All your data stays on your device — no account required.

01 — Getting Started

First launch & setup

When you open FincWin for the first time, a 9-step setup wizard walks you through your income, expenses, loans, savings, PIN protection, and storage preference. This takes about 2–3 minutes.

How to complete the setup wizard
1
Open FincWin in your browser. The welcome screen appears automatically on first visit. Click Get Started →.
2
Step 1 — Your name & currency. Enter your first name (optional) and select your home currency from the dropdown.
3
Step 2 — Income sources. Add each monthly income stream (salary, freelance, etc.) with a name and amount. Click + or press Enter for each one.
4
Step 3 — Monthly expenses. Add your recurring bills (rent, phone, subscriptions). Each gets a name, amount, frequency, due day, week slot, and optional receipt photo.
5
Step 4 — Loans & debts. Add any outstanding loans with balance, interest rate, and minimum payment. Skip if you have none.
6
Step 5 — Savings goals. Add savings accounts or goals with target amount, current balance, and monthly contribution.
7
Step 6 — Protect your data. Set a 6-digit PIN and an optional recovery passphrase. You can skip this and enable it later in Settings.
8
Step 7 — Save your data locally. Choose where your data lives: Local File (default), Google Drive, or Firebase cloud sync. You can change this any time in Settings.
9
Click Open Dashboard. Your dashboard is ready with this month pre-populated.
💡
Skip the wizard. On the welcome screen, click "Explore with demo data →" to load a rich sample profile instantly and see every feature in action.

04 — Budgeting & Expenses

Bills, budgets & envelopes

The Expenses tab is the heart of FincWin. Expenses are organised into 14 category envelopes and four week slots. Each envelope has a monthly cap (budget), and a colour-coded bar shows how fast you're spending it.

14 Category Envelopes
Banking, Telecom, Subscriptions, Auto, Utilities, Housing, Food/Meals, Entertainment, Fees, Health, Loan Pmt, Tuition, Savings, Other
Budget Caps
Set a monthly spending limit per category. The bar turns amber at 75% and red when over.
Recurring Rules
Quarterly and yearly expenses auto-added to qualifying months, managed in the collapsible Recurring Rules panel.
Auto-fill Recurring
One-click button detects bills from previous months and fills them into the current month automatically.
Multi-Currency Items
Log any expense in a foreign currency. FincWin converts it to your home currency automatically.
Receipt Photos
Attach an image receipt to any expense — tap the camera icon on the expense row.
Due Date Calendar
A visual calendar heatmap on the Expenses tab shows which bills fall on which days this month.
Category Tag Filter
Filter the expense list by category using the pill bar above the week slots.
Drag-and-Drop Reorder
Drag expense rows to reorder them within a week. Works with mouse or touch.
How to add an expense
1
Go to the Expenses tab.
2
Click the + button inside any week slot (Week 1–4), or press the global Quick Add button (floating button, bottom-right).
3
Enter the expense name, amount, and optional due day. The category is auto-detected from the name — you can override it.
4
To make it recurring, select a frequency (Monthly, Weekly, Bi-weekly, Quarterly, or Yearly).
5
Optionally attach a receipt photo by tapping the camera icon.
6
Click Save. The item appears in the chosen week and its category envelope updates immediately.
How to mark an expense as paid
1
Click the checkbox or the expense row's paid toggle. The row dims to show it's settled.
2
To mark all items in a week at once, click the ✓ All button at the top of the week slot.
💡
Unpaid items show in full colour; paid items are dimmed. The health score improves as you clear bills.
How to set a budget cap for a category
1
On the Expenses tab, click the Edit All button (pencil icon next to the Budget Envelopes heading).
2
The Bulk Budget modal opens showing all 14 categories. Enter a cap for each one you want to limit.
3
Click Save. Envelopes now display a spend bar relative to your cap.
💡
You can also access Bulk Budget via Settings → Budgets → Set Budgets.
How to delete an expense
1
Click the expense row to open it, then click the Delete (trash) button.
2
For bulk deletion: click the Select button on the week header to enter multi-select mode. Check items, then click Delete in the bulk action bar.
How to edit an expense amount inline
1
Click directly on the amount number in any expense row. It becomes an editable field.
2
Type the new amount and press Enter or click away to save.
How to auto-fill recurring bills
1
Navigate to the current month on the Expenses tab.
2
Click Auto-fill Recurring (appears when recurring bills from previous months are not yet in the current month).
3
FincWin adds all bills that appeared in 2+ prior months into the correct week based on their due day.

Confetti animation and a badge when a loan reaches $0.

1

2
Drag the slider to add an extra monthly amount (up to $2,000). The debt-free date and interest savings update in real time.
💡

How to use the Paydown Calculator
1
Scroll to the Paydown Calculator card on the Loans tab.
2
Select an existing loan from the Select Loan dropdown to pre-fill, or enter Custom values manually.
3
Enter principal, interest rate, minimum payment, target payoff period, and optional extra monthly amount.
4
Results show monthly payment, total paid, total interest, savings vs minimum-only, and payoff date. The full amortisation schedule appears below.
How to switch between Avalanche and Snowball strategy
1
On the Loans tab, find the Payoff Strategy toggle at the top.
2
Click Avalanche to prioritise highest-interest loans first (saves most money).
3
Click Snowball to prioritise smallest-balance loans first (fastest psychological wins).

15 — Cloud, Drive & File Sync

Your data, everywhere

By default FincWin stores data in your browser. For multi-device access choose from three sync options: a Local Folder (auto-save to disk), Google Drive (OAuth), or Firebase Cloud Sync (encrypted). All three are in Settings → Cloud & File.

How to link a local folder (Chrome / Edge)
1
Go to Settings → Cloud & File → Local Folder and click Choose folder.
2
Pick a folder (e.g. Documents). FincWin will auto-save fincwin-data.json there on every change.
3
On another browser or device, link the same folder and FincWin will load from that file.
💡
Other browsers (Safari, Firefox) without the File System Access API will prompt a manual download on demand instead of auto-saving.
How to connect Google Drive sync
1
Go to Settings → Cloud & File → Google Drive and click Connect.
2
Sign in to your Google account and grant permission. FincWin stores data in your Drive's app-private folder (not visible in your main Drive).
3
On a second device, connect the same Google account. Your data syncs automatically on each save.
How to enable Firebase cloud sync
1
Go to Settings → Cloud & File → Cloud Sync and click Set up.
2
Enter a passphrase. All data is encrypted with this passphrase before leaving your device. You must remember this — it cannot be recovered.
3
On a second device, open FincWin → Settings → Cloud & File, enable cloud sync, and enter the same passphrase. Your data will sync automatically.
⚠️
Remember your passphrase. If you forget it, your cloud data cannot be decrypted by anyone — including us. Store it safely.

17 — PIN & Encryption

Protecting your data

FincWin uses a 6-digit PIN lock and AES-GCM encryption. Your PIN is the key — enable it in Settings or via the lock icon in the top bar. A session token means you won't be re-asked on every page refresh within the same tab session.

How to set a PIN
1
Click the lock icon in the top bar, or go to Settings → Security → Set / Change PIN.
2
Enter a 6-digit PIN twice to confirm.
3
Optionally add a recovery passphrase (min. 8 characters). Write it down — it's the only way back in if you forget the PIN.
4
Click Save PIN. The app will require this PIN on each new browser session. Within the same session, the PIN is remembered until the auto-lock timeout expires.
How to configure auto-lock timeout
1
Go to Settings → Security → Auto-lock after.
2
Choose a timeout: 30 min, 1 hr, 4 hrs (default), 8 hrs, 1 day, or Never (tab close only).
3
After this period of inactivity the app locks and asks for your PIN again. Closing the browser tab always locks regardless of timeout.

24 — FAQ

Common questions

Is my data safe? Does FincWin send it anywhere?

By default, all data lives entirely in your browser's local storage and IndexedDB. Nothing is sent to any server. Cloud Sync (Firebase) and Google Drive sync are opt-in and encrypt all data before it ever leaves your device. AI Coach calls send only a financial summary — never raw transaction names.

I forgot my PIN — how do I get back in?

If you set a recovery passphrase when creating the PIN, use the Forgot PIN? Use recovery passphrase option on the lock screen (appears after 10 failed attempts). If not, you'll need to clear app data from your browser settings (this erases all FincWin data). This is why regular JSON backups are important.

Can I use FincWin offline?

Yes. FincWin is a Progressive Web App (PWA) with a service worker that caches all assets. After your first visit, it works fully offline. Install it to your home screen for the best experience.

How do I install FincWin to my phone?
1
iOS (Safari): Tap the Share button → Add to Home Screen.
2
Android (Chrome): Tap the three-dot menu → Install app or Add to Home Screen.
3

Will my data transfer if I clear my browser?

Clearing browser storage will erase local FincWin data. Always download a JSON backup before clearing. If you use Google Drive or Firebase Cloud Sync, your data is safe in the cloud and will re-sync on next open.

Why does my PIN get asked every time I refresh?

FincWin uses a session token in sessionStorage so the PIN is only required once per tab session. If you're still prompted on every refresh, go to Settings → Security → Skip PIN on page refresh and toggle it on. When that setting is off, the encrypted DEK is not persisted between loads, providing higher security at the cost of convenience.
