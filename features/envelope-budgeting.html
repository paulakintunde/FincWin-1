The problem

Spending categories tell you the past, not the plan

Most apps show you where money went after it's gone. That's a report, not a budget. By the time you see the overspend, it already happened.

Envelope budgeting flips it: you decide where each dollar goes first, then spend against the plan. FincWin keeps every envelope live as you go.

How it works

How Envelope Budgeting works in FincWin

  1. Set your income — your monthly take-home becomes the pool you assign from.
  2. Create envelopes — Housing, Food, Transport, Subscriptions and more, across 14 categories.
  3. Choose a frequency — weekly, fortnightly, monthly, quarterly or yearly caps per envelope.
  4. Spend against the plan — each envelope shows what's left in real time.
