The problem

Local-first is private — until you lose the device

Keeping data in your browser is private, but it also means a lost phone or cleared cache can take your budget with it. Most apps solve this by storing your data on their servers — trading privacy for safety.

FincWin refuses the trade. Your backup goes to your own Google Drive, encrypted, and you can restore it anywhere.

How it works

How Google Drive Backup works in FincWin

  1. Connect Google Drive — authorise once; FincWin only touches its own backup file.
  2. Back up — your data is encrypted and saved to your Drive.
  3. Switch devices — open FincWin anywhere and restore from Drive.
  4. Stay in control — the file is yours; delete it any time.
