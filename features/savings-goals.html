The problem

Saving without a target rarely sticks

“Save more” isn't a plan. Without a named goal, an amount and a date, money slips back into everyday spending.

FincWin gives each goal its own envelope, progress bar and timeline — so saving feels like progress, not deprivation.

How it works

How Savings Goals works in FincWin

  1. Name the goal — Emergency fund, new laptop, holiday, deposit.
  2. Set target & date — FincWin works out the monthly amount needed.
  3. Fund it — assign money each month, manually or as a sinking fund.
  4. Track progress — every goal shows percentage complete and time remaining.
