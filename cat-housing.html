Housing costs are more than
just your rent or mortgage.

01

Maintenance surprises that wipe your savings

The water heater breaks in November. The AC fails in July. Home repairs average $3,000–$5,000/year. Without a housing maintenance budget, every repair is a crisis.

02

HOA fees that creep up every year

Your HOA was $250/month when you moved in. It's $340 now. Special assessments add hundreds more without warning. If it's not tracked, it quietly grows into a significant expense.

03

The true cost of homeownership is never just the mortgage

Property tax, home insurance, PMI, maintenance reserve — homeowners routinely underestimate total housing costs by $400–$600/month. The envelope reveals the real number.
