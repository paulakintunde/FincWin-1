The real cost

Personal care costs more
than you account for.

01

The gym membership you barely use but can't cancel

$55/month, 12 months of guilt every time you don't go. That's $660/year for access you rarely use. It's easier to track — and cancel — when you can see exactly what you've paid.

02

Personal care products that accumulate invisibly

Shampoo, conditioner, moisturizer, supplements, skincare routine — the personal care aisle adds $60–$100 per month. It rarely gets counted because it comes out of "groceries."

03

Therapy and wellness that needs to be non-negotiable

$150/session, bi-weekly — that's $300/month. Without a clear personal care envelope, this vital spending gets crowded out by discretionary spending when money feels tight.
