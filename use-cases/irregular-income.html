A fixed monthly budget doesn't fit a variable paycheck

When income swings month to month, ‘budget $X for groceries’ falls apart. A big month feels like permission to spend; a small one feels like panic.

FincWin works differently: you fund priorities in order as money arrives, and stash the surplus so the quiet months are already covered.
