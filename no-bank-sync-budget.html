The problem

Bank sync means trusting a middleman with your login

Most budget apps connect through services like Plaid, which need your banking credentials and route your transaction data through their servers. For a lot of people that's a hard no.

FincWin doesn't sync with your bank at all. You stay in control of what goes in — by hand or by CSV — and nothing leaves your device.
