The real cost

Clothing purchases feel small.
The total never does.

01

Sale psychology costs more than full price

You saved $40 on that jacket. But you spent $120 you weren't planning to spend. The sale made you feel smart, but your envelope knows you went over. By $80.

02

Seasonal wardrobe restocks hit all at once

Spring cleaning leads to a spring shopping trip. One week in April you spent $340 on clothes. Your monthly clothing budget was $80. Nobody warned you that "just a few things" would add up like that.

03

Kids outgrow everything faster than your wallet can keep up

Two kids going up a size every six months means $200–$400 in clothing replacements twice a year. Without a dedicated envelope, kids' clothing silently eats into groceries and everything else.
